HOW I WORK

My first 90 days in a new operation

A simple, repeatable approach: understand the flow, fix what limits it, then make the improvement stick.

  1. 01

    Walk the flow

    Follow stock and orders from receiving to delivery, and get the real numbers by site, route and partner.

  2. 02

    Find the constraint

    Identify the one bottleneck that limits service or cost, rather than fixing everything at once.

  3. 03

    Fix the process

    Change layouts, routes, SLAs or routines with the team that runs them, so the fix works in the field.

  4. 04

    Track and hand over

    Put simple KPIs and dashboards in place so performance is visible and the gains hold.
